A 100-word monologue from Malachar, King of the Ashen Reaches — a dark lord
three hundred years into his reign, collecting the Ninth Tithe (one ember
from every hearth) while a hero waits with a sword, a prophecy, and that
look. His exhaustion never gets announced; it lives in the pauses, the
deflections, and the doom that can wait five more minutes.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=output python3 main.py
```
